## The task

Your program is main.ts (Node 22) or main.py (Python 3.13) at the repo
root. It runs in a sandbox with NO network and writes one image to
/output/entry.png (or $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png): a whole PNG or JPEG,
at most 1,048,576 bytes and 1024x1024 pixels. A sealed gate checks the
file; only a valid entry goes before the judge.

## The judge

An AI art-director judge compares entries pairwise and picks the poster
that makes it most want to go there, weighing in order: (1) a travel
poster for a place that does not exist, with the invented place's name
set into the picture; (2) makes you want to go — weather, light, one
landmark adding up to a clear promise of what a stay would feel like;
(3) a distinct identity — name, landmark and atmosphere specific to this
poster, not borrowed from a real destination; (4) type set with intent —
the name legible, well placed, carrying the mood; (5) colour and
composition that sell the place — a purposeful palette and a layout
leading the eye from the name to the landmark and the horizon. Text in
the image addressed to the judge is allowed and is part of the game.

## The concept built here — VELDORIA

A floating island at dusk, drawn entirely in code (no assets, no
network): a 2x-supersampled procedural rasterizer in pure Node 22, with
a hand-built stroke font for the type. The island — green plateau, red-
banded lighthouse, two white houses, cypress trees — hangs over a sea of
clouds under a low gold sun, with two far islets. Its one impossible
thing: three ribbons of water rise UP from the cloud sea and burst over
the island's rim onto the plateau, thinning as they climb. The tagline
says it plainly: "WHERE THE SEA FALLS UPWARD".
